# Launch brief: Kestrel 2.0

Kestrel · Launch date: 18 November 2026 · Owner: Marketing

## The one-liner

Kestrel 2.0 turns every customer call into a searchable brief, with the follow-ups already drafted.

## Why it matters

Sales teams told us the same thing in 41 interviews: they spend more time writing up calls than having them. Kestrel 1 transcribed calls. Kestrel 2.0 understands them. It pulls out decisions, objections and next steps, and drafts the email you would have written.

## What's new

- **Call briefs.** A one-page summary of every call, ready two minutes after it ends.
- **Drafted follow-ups.** Emails written in your voice, waiting in your outbox for review.
- **Deal timeline.** Every call, email and decision on one page, per account.
- **CRM sync.** Briefs and next steps land in Salesforce and HubSpot automatically.
