# Proposal: restoring the Pier Street warehouse

Prepared for the Pier Street Trust by Calloway & Reid Architects | 3 November 2026

## Our understanding

The Pier Street warehouse was built in 1891 to store grain and has stood empty since 2009. The Trust wants to reopen it as a public market on the ground floor with studios above, while keeping the brick shell, the timber roof trusses and the loading doors that face the harbour.

The building is listed, so every change to the street and harbour elevations will need consent. We have restored four listed industrial buildings on this coast and know the conservation officers well.

## What we propose

- Repair the brick and lime mortar, and rebuild the collapsed north parapet.
- Keep the original trusses, strengthened with steel plates where the survey shows rot.
- Open the ground floor into one hall, with stalls that can be cleared for events.
- Fit out twelve studios on the upper floors, lit by the existing loading doors.

> The warehouse should still read as a working building from the water. We will add as little as the new uses allow and make every addition easy to tell apart from the old fabric.
