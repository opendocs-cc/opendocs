# Your first week at Tidewater

Team: Clinical operations | For: New nursing and front-desk staff | Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Tidewater Clinic. This guide covers what happens in your first five days, who to ask, and the few rules we never bend. Keep it handy; you will not remember everything on day one, and nobody expects you to.

## Day one

- Collect your badge and scrubs from reception before 8:00.
- Meet your preceptor, who will shadow you for the whole first week.
- Complete the infection control module in the learning portal.

## Days two to four

- Work alongside your preceptor on morning clinics.
- Learn the triage flow and practise it on two supervised patients a day.
- Book a 20-minute check-in with your team lead for Thursday.

## Day five

You will run a short clinic with your preceptor in the room. Afterwards, the two of you will agree on what you can do independently from week two.

## Rules we never bend

1. Wash your hands before and after every patient, no exceptions.
2. Two identifiers before every medication: name and date of birth.
3. If something feels wrong, stop and ask. Nobody here will be annoyed.
