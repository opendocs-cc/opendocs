# Q3 2026 investor update

Prepared for: Limited partners | Period: July to September 2026 | Status: Final

Halvard Capital closed the quarter with three new platform investments and a stronger cash position than planned. This update covers portfolio performance, the deals we made, and what we need from you before year end.

## Highlights

- Deployed 41% of Fund II, ahead of the 35% target for this point in the fund's life.
- Two portfolio companies raised follow-on rounds led by outside investors.
- Net IRR across Fund I stands at 18.4%, up from 16.9% last quarter.

## New investments

**Ostra Energy** builds battery storage for regional grid operators. We led the Series B alongside two strategic investors and took a board seat.

**Tern Analytics** sells forecasting software to mid-size retailers. Revenue grew 2.6x in the last twelve months.
