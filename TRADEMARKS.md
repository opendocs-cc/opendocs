**Running it for your own team is fine as it is.** A self-hosted instance
(see [SELF_HOSTING.md](SELF_HOSTING.md)) that you run for yourself or your
company, unmodified or with small changes, can keep the OpenDocs name and
logo. It runs on your own domain, so nobody mistakes it for opendocs.cc.

**If you share a modified version or offer it to others** (a fork, a
distribution, a service for people outside your company):

- Don't call it OpenDocs or use a name that could be mistaken for it.
- Remove the OpenDocs logos and icons (`apps/web/public/opendocs-*`,
  `favicon*`, `og-image*`, `apps/web/app/icon.png` and the logo assets in
  the docs).
- Replace the legal pages (`apps/web/app/terms`, `privacy`,
  `refund-policy` and `components/legal`), which describe opendocs.cc's
  service and company, with your own.

In every case, don't point it at opendocs.cc, api.opendocs.cc or
mcp.opendocs.cc, send email as opendocs.cc, or present it as the service at
opendocs.cc.

Saying that your project is "based on OpenDocs" or "works with OpenDocs" is
fine.
